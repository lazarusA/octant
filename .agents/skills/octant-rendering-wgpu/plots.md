# WGPU renderers (`src/plots/`)

Reference for this skill, moved from `AGENTS.md` (which keeps the rules most tasks need); update it with the code it describes.

- **WGPU Renderers (`src/plots/`)**:
  - **Label images (`src/plots/labels.rs`, `shaders/labels.wgsl`)**: `LabelRenderer` draws a heatmap renderer's data (group 0, read as `u32` ids) through its own group 1 (sorted `(id, RGBA)` palette, fill or outline, opacity): recorded colors, else a PCG-hashed color; 0 transparent; outlines one screen pixel wide. `labels::label_color` mirrors the colors on the CPU for the hover. Built in `rebuild.rs` for label layers (the pipeline once, `with_palette` per variable; no 3D renderers), painted by `HeatmapCallback` with its `labels: Option<LabelDraw>` from `paint_layer.rs`; the heatmap and label shaders share `shaders/common/quad.wgsl` (`QuadUniforms`, `vs_main`, `quad_cell`); `labels_tests.rs` validates the pipeline on a GPU.
  - **Multi-channel composites on the GPU (`src/plots/channels.rs`, `channel_params.rs`, `shaders/common/channel_blend.wgsl`, `shaders/channels.wgsl`)**: the blend is one WGSL include (`blend_cell`; `channel_value` turns it into the packed value `pack_or_nan` gives CPU-blended composites, so heights, colors and missing data read it unchanged), bound at group 2 by every pipeline that blends (`channels::bind_group_layout`; `ChannelRenderer::bind`; its zeroed parameters draw no channel): the heatmap's channel pipeline (per fragment) and the bake (`plots/channel_bake.rs`, `shaders/channel_bake.wgsl`: a compute pass writing each cell's packed color into the surface's, sphere's or point cloud's own data buffer from their callbacks' `prepare` (`Mesh3DCallback::channels`, `PointCloudCallback::channels`, and the 3D coastlines' own copy, `Coastline3DCallback::channels`), sampling a thinned mesh grid's cells (`Thinning`, `Layer::mesh_thinning`), only when the parameters, the planes (`planes_version`, bumped by every upload) or the target changed (`ChannelRenderer::baked`; targets named by a never-reused `BakeId`, so the memo holds no GPU resource; a mesh's, 3D coastlines' and point cloud's `BakeSlot` renews its id on every raw upload (`update_data`, `update_data_range`), so the next frame bakes again, `channel_bake_tests.rs` `a_raw_upload_bakes_the_mesh_again`), so their frames cost what CPU-blended ones do; `channel_bake_tests.rs`, `app/surface_blend_bench_tests.rs` `cargo test --release --lib surface_blend_timed -- --ignored --nocapture`). A point cloud or volume layer's multi-channel composite (not GeoTIFF, the channel axis not its Z, every channel of the block, and a volume's padded validity words, within `MAX_GPU_STORAGE_BUFFER_BYTES`) keeps its block's raw channels (`app/block_loading/projection_channels_3d.rs`: `gpu_channel_count`, `sync_channel_volume`, `channel_slabs`) in `LayerData::channel_volume` (`ChannelVolume`, the channels stacked along Z, written by `commit_volume_slab` at the volume's places, ring planes included, and uploaded with the point cloud's dirty planes, `ChannelRenderer::write_cells`) while `volume` holds the selected channel's values; each channel's stretch is the first slab's after the volume is allocated, and the volume key (`target_desc` `ch=gpu{first}+{count}`) names the channel window and leaves the display out, so display changes keep the volume and a new channel window reallocates it, a 2D projection drops it (`project_channel_planes`, `clear_3d`). The volume raymarcher takes it baked into its textures (`plots/channel_bake_volume.rs`, `shaders/channel_bake_volume.wgsl`; its uniforms, validity words and plane marks kept per target in `channel_bake_targets.rs` (the bind group made per bake, holding no texture), from `VolumeCallback::channels` through `VolumeRenderer::bake_channels`): `Rgba8Unorm` colors written as a storage texture and validity words copied into the `R8Unorm` texture (not a WebGPU storage format), as `encode_rgba` encodes CPU-blended ones (missing voxels filled from their valid 26 neighbors, skipped where no plane around them holds data, `mark_planes`), then `VolumeTextures::mark_baked` (a new data version for the frame cache; validity always read); the per-frame upload sends the channels' planes instead of encoding (`volume_upload.rs` `upload_channels`); `channel_bake_volume_tests.rs`, `app/volume_blend_bench_tests.rs` `cargo test --release --lib volume_blend_timed -- --ignored --nocapture`. The volume hover reads voxels through `Layer::gpu_blended_voxel` (`blend_samples`). A heatmap, surface or sphere layer's multi-channel composite (not RGB/CMYK, not HEALPix, at most `MAX_CHANNELS` (16) planes within one storage buffer, `limits::max_storage_bytes`, so in the browser within its 512 MB budget; desktop and WebGPU alike) uploads its block's raw channel planes once (`app/block_loading/projection_channels.rs` `project_channel_planes`, `each_plane`; the layer's matrix then holds the first channel's values, for its grid) to `LayerRenderers::channels`, drawn over the heatmap renderer's quad like label images (`HeatmapCallback::channels`); `ChannelParams::of` builds the drawn channels' tints, stretches (the plot's, `CompositeStretch::of_channel`) and curves (`ChannelCurve::gpu_terms`) from the layer's style every frame without allocating, and `channels.wgsl` blends them as `ChannelAccumulator` does. Display changes (ticks, tints, windows, curves) then reload nothing on the base layer (`channel_display_reloads`; overlays still reload, their ticks pick the channels they read); detail tiles upload their own planes (`detail_channels`) and keep through display changes (one too big for them, blended on the CPU, is dropped and asked again, `channel_display_changed`) (`detail/want.rs` `styled` leaves the style out of their context). The hover reads such cells with `blend_pixel` (`Layer::gpu_blended_cell`, `ui/hover/detail.rs` `tile_reading`; the surface and sphere raycasts through `ui/hover/smooth.rs` `value_at`, their hit cache keyed on the channel display). Tests: `channels_tests.rs` (shader against the CPU blend on a GPU), `app/channel_gpu_tests.rs` and `app/detail/channel_tests.rs` (over `test_support::render_state`, a headless GPU render state), `app/channel_blend_bench_tests.rs` `cargo test --release --lib channel_blend_timed -- --ignored --nocapture` (CPU against GPU at TESSERA's coarsest RGB level).
  - Colormapped pipelines bind the shared colormap atlas (`colormap_atlas.rs`, 256×K `Rgba8Unorm`, stored in egui-wgpu `CallbackResources`) at `@group(1)`: add `colormap_atlas::bind_group_layout(device)` to the pipeline layout, call `colormap_atlas::prepare(device, queue, callback_resources)` in the callback's `prepare` (uploads rows changed anywhere in the frame) and `colormap_atlas::bind(rpass, callback_resources)` in `paint`. Sample colors in WGSL with `sample_plot_colormap(color, t)` (honors `reverse`).
  - **Volume raymarcher (`src/plots/volume/`)**: voxels live in 3D textures (`textures.rs`, created and written by `texture_writes.rs`: `R32Float` values, `Rgba8Unorm` for RGB composites, `R8Unorm` validity; uploads cover whole Z planes plus one neighbor plane each side; chunks without missing voxels upload straight from the volume, others are encoded in parallel by `encode.rs` (missing voxels take their valid 26-neighbor average so trilinear filtering stays smooth; the filtered validity's 0.5 level is the missing-data boundary). Rays step `quality` samples per voxel (at most `MAX_SAMPLES` = 384 per ray, `main.wgsl`) with interleaved-gradient jitter. Colors come from the shared colormap atlas (`@group(1)`, bound in the raymarch pass via `colormap_atlas::bind_group`). DVR (mode 0, `dvr.wgsl`) samples trilinearly; its opacity is `dvr_opacity` (`t^(1/Density)` of the scale position, floored at 0.01; Density default 3) per reference step (`reference_step(dir)`: `REFERENCE_STEP`, 1/64, of the box's depth through its center along the ray's direction, at most of its longest side 1; computed once per ray as `Ray::step_refs`), so a cube is as before, a box thin along one axis (a 2D map drawn as a one-slice volume) is as opaque through its thickness as a cube through its depth instead of letting the canvas show through, and along its long sides as see-through as before; corrected for the actual step so it is independent of quality. `pipeline.rs` builds two shader variants from `src/plots/shaders/volume/`: hardware filtering when the device has `FLOAT32_FILTERABLE` (requested when available by `src/plots/device.rs`), else an exact eight-tap filter. Ring-buffer shifts use a Repeat sampler with logical coordinates clamped to edge texel centers. DVR shading (`shading.wgsl`) uses world-space tetrahedral gradients (stencils kept off the box faces), viewer-facing normals and a camera-following headlight (`lit_diffuse`: two-sided, `LIGHT_AMBIENT` 0.35 + `LIGHT_DIFFUSE` 0.9 · |n·l|, so lighting never dims a viewer-facing surface; the classic modes keep the uniforms' own); opaque hits are bisected, and a volume cut open at the entry face shades with the face normal. Opaque DVR (Transparency off) draws its surface where values enter the color range, or with the Settings Surface row's Value (`VolumePlotConfig::surface`, `VolumeSurface`; `SURFACE_VALUE`) where they reach `isovalue` (set to the middle of the data's extent when first chosen), colored by the colormap, whatever the color range. Transparent DVR lighting (`lighting` uniform, "Lighting" checkbox, off by default) blends diffuse-lit color in by gradient strength and never changes opacity; only samples still showing through (`a * (1 - alpha_acc)`) read the gradient. Modes 1-7 (`classic.wgsl`, `projections.wgsl`, `rgba.wgsl`) keep the original algorithms on nearest voxels with straight alpha, premultiplied in `fs_main`; change them only on request. MIP (mode 1) visits every voxel its ray crosses (`walk.wgsl`: an exact 3D DDA in logical texel space, the stored texel following the ring shift without a modulo; `walk_begin`, `walk_step`, `walk_exit`, `walk_face_normal`) instead of fixed samples, so no maximum is stepped over, skips the attenuation `exp` at attenuation 0 and stops once its maximum passes cmax; its hover walks the same voxels (`ui/hover/voxel_walk.rs` `walk_voxels`, `mip_hit`). The Categorical Label Surface (mode 4, `rgba.wgsl` `label_iso`) walks them too: a surface wherever the drawn value changes (`label_color`: values in the color range, outside it with their clip color on, missing voxels with the NaN color on), lit by the face the walk entered through (`walk_face_normal`; the entry face at the volume's edge or a cut), opaque or, with Transparency on, each surface at the layer's opacity (kept by `get_volume_uniform_params`, `support.rs`), so boundaries between classes show through; its hover takes the first drawn voxel (`label_hit`). Rays skip empty space (`volume/blocks.rs`, `blocks.wgsl`): per block of 8³ stored voxels an `Rgba32Float` texel (binding 4) holds the min and max valid value over the block and its 26 neighbors (so trilinear taps, missing-voxel fills and gradients stay covered) and whether any voxel is missing, computed on the CPU from per-plane stats of each block column, so an upload (a playback step's plane) scans only its own planes and reduces the block rows holding them (`Blocks::update`, `plane_stats`, `own_row`; composites always open); DVR jumps its samples past blocks drawing nothing (`next_open_sample`: `OPEN_DRAWN`, or `OPEN_VALUE` for a Value surface), MIP (`OPEN_ABOVE` its running maximum, not with attenuation) and label surface walks jump to the block's exit (`walk_skip_block`, `block_exit`; a block the ring seam splits is left at the volume's face); `gpu_skipping_blocks_changes_nothing` renders every mode with and without skipping, `bench_tests.rs` `cargo test --release --lib volume_mode_timed -- --ignored --nocapture` times the modes. Output is dithered and premultiplied      - Rendering goes through a cached offscreen frame (`frame.rs` `FrameKey`: uniforms, data upload version, colormap generation, size; `render.rs`): the raymarch reruns only when the key changes and is blitted into egui's pass otherwise, at half resolution while view_interacting (drag or zoom; `app.nav.view_interacting`). Each mode has its own pipeline (WGSL `override ALGORITHM`, built on first use). Tests: `tests.rs` (CPU, uniform layout vs WGSL), `gpu_tests.rs` (filter parity, step-independent opacity, seam, missing data, frame cache).
  - Animated volumes: blocks update the plotted layer's CPU volume (`LayerData::volume`) and mark changed Z planes (`src/app/block_loading/volume_upload.rs`); `flush_volume_uploads` sends them once per frame, before painting, to the renderer the active plot shows (the other keeps its pending planes). Cache hits re-project the other resident blocks only after the volume was reset (`LayerData::volume_allocations`). A volume whose Z axis is the animated one (time, lat, lon) is a sliding window (`src/app/block_loading/volume_ring/`: `mod.rs`, `window.rs` the window geometry): the `depth` steps from the current one counted around the series (after the last step come the first ones, looping or not) in a ring buffer turned one plane per step from the last written window (`RingPlanes::anchor`, `front_plane`, so the loop from the last step to the first is one turn too; read by the writes, by `get_volume_shifts` through `ring_shift` and by the volume hover through `ring_plane_step`), each step writing only the planes the window gained from the resident blocks holding them (`fill_ring`, `find_covering_block`; `LayerData::ring` records each plane's step, `write_volume_slab` its `ring_slot`), planes not loaded yet empty, never a whole block at once; playback and the playing lookahead wait for the window's last plane (`ring_lead`), the lookahead wrapping to the first chunks and a paused one reading the window's chunks first (`ring_chunks`); a flipped Z and animated X or Y axes keep whole blocks (`LayerData::last_volume_block` skips rewriting the same one); `volume_step_tests.rs`. The Plot button calls `plot_selection` (clamps the step into the selected animated range); the playback timer calls `advance_playback` (`src/app/block_loading/step_nav.rs`), which loads the next resident step with `load_selected_variable_block` (it may run past the selected range); while another variable is only selected or a new plot's block is pending, it does so against the plotted selection (`staging.rs` `with_plotted_selection`; the step buttons and timeline alike, `request_step_or_load`, which fetches no step while a new plot's block (another variable or window) loads, `new_plot_pending`), and the new plot replaces the view at its requested step (`LoadState::pending_target_step`) with the selection it was requested from (`LoadState::requested`, so a variable staged meanwhile stays staged) when the block arrives. Arriving blocks are projected only when they match the latest request (`key_matches_view`) and, via `block_in_view`, the current step.
  - Transparency of meshes and point clouds (`src/plots/oit/` `Transparency`, resolved by `transparency_mode` in `src/app/state/alpha_state.rs` from the Transparency checkbox (`app.plot_configs.plot_transparency`) and translucent colors): `Off` writes depth; `Oit` is weighted blended order-independent transparency (`state.rs` `OitState`, held per renderer in an `OitSlot` and built on first use: an opaque pass (`fs_opaque`, alpha >= 0.995, writes depth) and a translucent pass (`fs_oit`, additive `Rgba16Float` sum and `R16Float` revealage) into offscreen targets during `prepare`, `frame.rs` composite into egui's pass during `paint`, built like the volume blit by `src/plots/fullscreen.rs` `frame_pipeline` (shared `shaders/common/fullscreen.wgsl` triangle, the frame shader's `fs_main`); shared WGSL in `shaders/common/oit.wgsl`, whose depth weight (capped at 300 so the half-float sum holds hundreds of near layers) inverts the camera's `near`/`far` planes, `camera3d.wgsl` `Camera`); frames (about 18 bytes per pixel) are freed when a renderer draws without OIT (`OitSlot::release`, and `release_idle_oit_frames` for 3D renderers not on screen); frames are cached (`OitState` keeps the key it rendered: `oit/inputs.rs` `FrameInputs` of each mesh and point cloud renderer, their uniforms, a data version bumped by every upload and channel bake, the colormap generation and the pipeline, with the size), so repaints that change none of them only composite; `oit/tests.rs` `an_unchanged_frame_is_not_rendered_again`. Without OIT (`Off`, `NoDepthWrite`) they draw through the same key into a cached offscreen frame of their own pipelines (`plots/frame_cache.rs` `FrameSlot`; `render_frame` and `paint_frame` pick a renderer's OIT or cached frame, freeing the other, and both without a colormap atlas), drawn onto egui's pass with its depth (`shaders/frame_cache_blit.wgsl` writes `frag_depth`), so 3D coastlines drawn after it stay hidden behind the plot; `frame_cache_tests.rs`, `app/frame_cache_bench_tests.rs` `cargo test --release --lib frame_cache_timed -- --ignored --nocapture` (benches that time geometry turn the camera each run, `orbit`); `NoDepthWrite` is the fallback when `oit::supported` is false. Plot shaders keep their color in `shade()` with `fs_main`, `fs_opaque` and `fs_oit` entry points. Every mesh and point cloud pipeline (egui pass and OIT) is built by `oit/pipeline.rs` `build_pipeline` from a `Variant` (`Variant::egui`, `OPAQUE`, `ACCUMULATE`); draw calls and callbacks live in `mesh_draw.rs` and `point_cloud_draw.rs`; a sphere or surface reads colors from its `data_buffer` and heights from bind group 2's `height_buffer` (`mesh_heights.rs` `HeightSlot`, `Mesh3DCallback::heights`: its own data, or the base layer's under an overlay draped on it, re-bound when the base renderer is rebuilt (the overlay's frame key holds the base's id and inputs), its own heights while the two grids differ in size; `Mesh3DUniforms::height_range`/`height_flags` the base's range, flip and composite bits; HEALPix corners averaged per buffer, `healpix_corner_cells`/`healpix_corner_mean`; `mesh_heights_tests.rs`) (`point_cloud_types.rs` vertex and uniform structs); tests in `oit/tests.rs` (their scene in `oit/test_scene.rs`); plot GPU tests get their device from `src/plots/test_gpu.rs`.
  - Plot renderers (`HeatmapRenderer`, `LineRenderer`, `Mesh3DRenderer`, `VolumeRenderer`, `PointCloudRenderer`) share no trait: each owns its GPU buffers and upload methods (`update_data` over `&[f32]`, and its own range and channel uploads), and is drawn by its egui paint callback (`HeatmapCallback`, `LineCallback`, `Mesh3DCallback`, `VolumeCallback`, `PointCloudCallback`; `prepare` uploads and bakes, `paint` draws), built per layer in `app/pipeline/paint_layer.rs`; hover reads live in `src/ui/hover/`. (An earlier `PlotRenderer` trait, whose methods nothing called, was removed.) Plot-specific canvas interaction (drag, pan, zoom, scroll, view reset) and each plot's resolution demand belong to the `crate::app::controllers::PlotController` implementations (`HeatmapController`, `LineController`, and `Orbit3DController` for the surface, sphere, volume and point cloud) via `controller_for(plot_type)`.
  - **3D camera (`src/plots/camera.rs`)**: `OrbitCamera` (rotations, `distance`, `target`, aspect), built each frame by `NavigationState::orbit_camera` (`sphere_rotation_x/y`, `sphere_zoom` as the distance, `orbit_target`; the sphere always looks at its center), is the one camera of the surface, sphere, volume, point cloud and 3D coastlines and of the hover (`ui/hover/camera.rs` `Camera3D`: `cast_ray`, `project_point`). Its `CameraUniform` (view matrix, object-space eye, `FOV_SCALE`, aspect, near plane shrinking with the distance, far) is the first field of every 3D uniform block, mirrored by WGSL `Camera` in `shaders/common/camera3d.wgsl` (`camera_view`, `camera_view_normal`, `camera_to_object_dir`, `camera_project`; `oit_accumulate` inverts depth with its planes); the volume raymarches from `camera.eye`. `Orbit3DController` pans by moving the target across the view (`screen_offset`, within `TARGET_LIMIT`) and zooms toward the point under the pointer at the target's depth (down to `min_distance`); the sphere's pan turns the globe and its zoom divides the height above it, turning slower as the camera comes closer. Never project in a shader or the hover by hand; read the camera. `camera_tests.rs`, `uniform_layout_tests.rs` `uniform_layouts_match_wgsl`.
  - **Surface and sphere heights**: the surface, the sphere and their 3D coastlines compute heights with the one `normalized_height` of `src/plots/shaders/common/height.wgsl` (included by `assemble_plot_with_coords_shader!` and the coastline shader), mirrored on the CPU by `src/utils/height.rs` `HeightScale` for the hover raycasts: the color range position, or for RGB composites the length of the packed color (black 0, white 1); `flip` (`MeshPlotConfig::surface_flip_height`/`sphere_flip_height`, "Flip heights" in the plot options, read through `MeshPlotConfig::heights`) puts the top value at zero and the others above it. Smooth modes place each value at its cell center (`smooth_corner` in `height.wgsl`; the first and last vertices of an axis stretch to its edges, global longitudes wrap), so they line up with steps, blocks, the heatmap and the hover. The sphere and its hover place regional grids through `CoordinateGrid::sphere_coord_mode`: bounds that are no degrees (projected coordinates, in metres) wrap the whole globe like global grids. Packed composite values are never interpolated between vertices: the vertex shader unpacks them into the `rgb` varying, which `shade()` uses in composite mode.
  - **Buffer limits (`src/plots/limits.rs`)**: `device.rs` requests the adapter's storage binding and buffer sizes (`raised`, never lowering egui's defaults) and `OctantApp::new` records them (`set_from_device`); `max_storage_elements` (the device's limit, WebGPU's 128 MiB default until a device is known, capped at 512 MB in the browser) decides whether a 2D heatmap uploads whole or through a pyramid (`needs_pyramid`, the heatmap data buffer, the Resampling note). Volumes and point clouds (built together), the sphere and surface (built with every 2D plot), default selections and batching keep `common::MAX_GPU_STORAGE_BUFFER_BYTES` (128 MiB). `limits_tests.rs`.
