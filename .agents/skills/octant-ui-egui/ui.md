The cut reaches every plot as one `plots::slice::SliceUniform` (`Slicer::uniform`, or `uniform_in(flip, heights)` for renderers whose axes run the other way (the volume's, `slice::VOLUME_FLIP`) and the surface's or sphere's w range as normalized heights (`Slicer::height_range`, open when the plot draws no relief: `slice::FLAT_HEIGHT`, the gizmo's flat rule)): `lo.w` the mode, the box `lo..=hi`, planes at `lo.xyz`, negative hidden; placed right after the camera in each uniform block and read through `shaders/common/slice.wgsl` (`slice_box_keeps`, `slice_box_keeps_uv`, `slice_planes_keep`: the one cell a plane falls in). Cells are kept by their centers in `[lo, hi]` everywhere (`index_ranges` too). The volume's ray slab is the cut box (cut faces shade as entry faces) and its planes draw opaque orthoslices of the exact voxel, hiding what the hover hides (`volume/planes.wgsl`); point clouds cull points by cell center in the vertex shader; the surface and sphere discard fragments by `slice_at` (cell fractions and normalized height, HEALPix by lon/lat), their blocks kept or cut whole by cell center and value; coastlines (2D and 3D) by u and v over their cell-edge bounds only when the Slicer row's Cut coastlines is on (`Slicer::cut_coastlines`, off by default, kept across modes; `for_coastlines`); the heatmap, label and channel quads by `outside_quad` over the whole data (`tile_bounds`; label outlines trace the cut). `slice::volume_world`/`volume_fraction` place fractions in the volume and point cloud box for the gizmo and the hover alike. Hovers match (`ui/hover/slice_hit.rs`: rays within `cut_bounds`, `plane_hit`, voxels picked as the shaders do; surface and sphere skip cut cells by center and by value height, `Slicer::keeps_height` (`surface_slice.rs`, `sphere_slice.rs`; the hit cache keys on `Slicer::hash_into`); the heatmap reads nothing cut away); while a handle is dragged the view counts as interacting (volumes preview at half resolution). Heights per mode come from one `utils::height::height_factor` (surface 0.8, steps 0.6, sphere 0.4, none flat; the shaders' copies pinned by `shaders_use_the_same_height_factors`), read by the hover, `slice::has_relief` (`app/pipeline/slice_params.rs` `mesh_slice`) and the gizmo alike (`placement_tests.rs` checks gizmo and shaders agree on flat plots). `volume/slice_gpu_tests.rs`, `plots/slice_gpu_tests.rs` (heatmap, surface, sphere, blocks cut whole, point cloud box and planes, through their paint callbacks), `ui/hover/slice_hit_tests.rs`, `ui/hover/slice_cell_tests.rs`; offscreen GPU frames of a paint callback, `plots/test_frame.rs`. Irregular heatmaps (coordinate lookup tables) cut where cells are drawn, not by cell index. Plan: `SLICE_PLAN.md`.
