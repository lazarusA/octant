# Bundled colormap licenses

Generated by `cargo run --bin fetch_colormaps` from the original projects listed per family (pinned in `src/bin/fetch_colormaps/families/urls.rs`). Sources that cannot be pinned to a commit are verified by MD5 checksum. Each family keeps the license of its original authors.

Changes made by Octant to every colormap: color values are quantized to 8-bit sRGB and resampled into 256-entry lookup tables for display; maps defined by Matplotlib segment data or functions are sampled at 256 points as Matplotlib does. No other changes are made.

**Good-faith notice.** We have made a good-faith effort to identify the authors and licenses of all third-party software, fonts and colormap data included in Octant, and to comply with their terms.
